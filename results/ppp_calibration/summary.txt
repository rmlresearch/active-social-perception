=== Interpretation (added after the run; calibration episodes only, test set untouched)
- The replay reproduces the live measurement exactly (results/ppp_accuracy), so the offline replay is faithful.
- Detection: measured 0.728 vs the filter's assumed 0.720 (0.9 x its occlusion model): ALREADY CALIBRATED.
  The "detection only" / "calibrated" variants above are INVALID: p_detect_model replaces the base 0.9, to which
  the filter's occlusion model is applied again (effective ~0.58), which the measurement does not support. The
  consistent base value is 0.9 x 0.728 / 0.720 = 0.91, i.e. no change.
- Refill: the likelihood worsens monotonically with relax -> 0 (no change).
- On the calibration episodes the filter is NOT biased low: near the robot outside the view it expects 0.585
  untracked pedestrians per step vs 0.532 true (0.463 never detected + 0.069 detected before and pruned).
- Cause of the test-set underestimate: crowd-density shift. Pedestrians per frame, 2nd half / 1st half of each
  recording: zara01 1.14x, zara02 1.53x, eth 2.27x, hotel 1.66x (Q2/Q1: 0.71, 1.08, 0.91, 0.80). The prior is fitted
  on the 1st half and the test episodes are in the 2nd; the online crowd multiplier recovers only part of the shift.
- Minor: 58% of the tracks pruned for low existence (171 on calibration episodes) belonged to people still present;
  they are recycled with their residual existence (~0.05) only.
